02

Tape or extension?

Two ways to get length. Which one is right depends on how long you want to keep the set — and how much you do with your hands.

Tape

Lighter on the nail and quicker to fit. For a few weeks of length, or a set you want to try before committing.

Extension

Built to stay. For the long almond or square you want to still have next month.

Don't pick from a description. Send me the set you've saved and I'll tell you which one gets you there.

03

How a booking works

  1. Send me a picture. Whatever you've saved from Instagram or TikTok. Stuck between two? Send both — we'll pick together.
  2. I confirm your slot. The day, the time, and what it will cost, before you leave home.
  3. Come through. I'll have the table ready and the lamp warm. Plan to be here a while — a good set isn't a quick one.
